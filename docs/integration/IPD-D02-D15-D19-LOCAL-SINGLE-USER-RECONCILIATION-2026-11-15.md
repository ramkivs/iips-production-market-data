## D15 — Historical S1-owner portfolio disposition

> ### D15 — Historical S1-owner portfolio disposition
>
> - **What / why:** Choose whether historical portfolios owned by `ipd-owner-ramki-personal` are initially separate from D02, exposed through an explicit alias/read projection, or re-keyed to `owner-4dc756b3`. The high-level ownership direction does not decide where existing rows live.
> - **Alternatives and consequences:** (a) defer/keep separate—safest and no data movement, but D02 cannot claim complete history; (b) a fully specified alias/read projection—avoids rewriting keys but adds dual-owner query, authorization, provenance and lifecycle complexity; (c) exact audited re-key—aligns storage with D02 owner but changes existing keys and needs inventory, collision policy, backup and separate authority.
> - **Technical recommendation — NOT APPROVED:** Do not move rows now. PA should first choose defer as the immediate safe state; decide alias versus re-key only after read-only inventory. If re-key is later chosen, migrate only rows proven to belong to the exact S1 personal identity/tenant; never merge.
> - **Risk / S1 effect / approval:** Defer means historical portfolios may not appear in D02 until a later decision. Alias can expose query leaks; re-key can affect recovery/idempotency. S1 identity itself stays unchanged, but row owners might change under (c). **Level C disposition now; separate Level E approval for any inventory/movement.**

**2. Applicability classification.** **UNRESOLVED — EVIDENCE REQUIRED**. The approved overall-owner direction does not establish whether the live database contains historical S1-owner portfolios or how such rows become visible under D02. At minimum, record the exact stored ID and owner/tenant, operator and time, intended treatment, factual basis, supporting local evidence references, and any uncertainty or conflicts, without collecting account credentials. An uncontradicted statement based on direct knowledge may support identifying personal ownership, subject to evidence review; an unexplained assertion or matching key alone is insufficient when ownership is ambiguous. Neither an ownership attestation nor a label by itself establishes an unrecorded broker origin. Retained source evidence may establish a source fact only to the extent it actually supports it.

Treat the exact S1 owner/tenant key as a necessary candidate filter, not conclusive proof that an arbitrary row belongs to the intended portfolio: [migration 001](../../src/persistence/migrations/001_initial_schema.ts) does not link a portfolio to an S1 membership. ## D16 — Live inventory and collision rules

> - **Alternatives and consequences:** (a) read-only inventory/manifest, exact ownership proof, batch-wide stop on any inconsistency; (b) resolve collisions by merging/overwriting/choosing a winner; (c) skip conflicting rows and partially migrate. (b)/(c) risk silent data loss, mixed ownership and partial history.
> - **Technical recommendation — NOT APPROVED:** Require a separately authorized, read-only inventory first; exact table/FK/row count/key/null/digest report, source records/links if present, `foreign_key_check`, and hashed manifest. Any collision, orphan, unknown owner, ambiguous row or restore receipt stops the whole proposed batch; no merge, overwrite, winner selection or partial migration. PA decides disposition after seeing evidence.
> - **Risk / S1 effect / approval:** Actual counts, candidate IDs and collisions are unknown; no safe final re-key choice can be made without live inventory. No S1 change during read-only inventory. **Level C preconditions now; Level E read-only authority before inventory; separate Level E migration authority later.**

**2. Applicability classification.** **UNRESOLVED — EVIDENCE REQUIRED**. The checked-in migration list is a repository schema inventory, not an inventory of the operator’s live store. An actual, separately authorized read-only inventory is required before a migration decision involving historical rows or a claim of complete historical coverage; it is not required merely to discuss a schema or keep rows unchanged.

**3. Minimum sufficient requirement.** A later inventory, if specifically authorized, should produce a concise local record of:

1. **Target and observation:** the exact database/environment and scope, observation time, actual SQLite/runtime version and a consistent read-only snapshot or quiescent view. Record the method and evidence that it did not mutate the live store; account for the actual journal/WAL and sidecars as needed. A copied main file alone is not automatically a consistent snapshot. Keep sensitive local values out of public documentation.
2. **Actual schema and ledger:** observed tables, columns, keys, collations, indexes, constraints and FK edges; ordered `_migration_ledger` IDs, checksums and timestamps compared with the applicable checked-in SQL-text checksums. Record missing, unknown, out-of-order or mismatching entries and actual schema differences. Proposed owner/source/link tables are included only if actually present; their presence is not assumed.
3. **Identities and portfolios:** actual S1 binding/membership keys, attachment, tenant, owner and statuses; all existing S1-owner and D02-owner portfolios plus relevant other owner/tenant groups, portfolio IDs/statuses/current revisions, including tombstones and foreign or malformed rows. Identify exact S1-owner candidates and existing D02 destinations, and record counts and enough keys/digests to make the observation reviewable. A matching key is a candidate, not proof of intended personal ownership.
4. **Children and history:** for each candidate and affected destination, inventory `portfolio_revisions`, `portfolio_holdings`, `portfolio_contributions` and `portfolio_restore_receipts`, including revision/holding/contribution keys, pointers, row counts, provenance and NULL patterns, and relevant receipt/idempotency fingerprints. Include any actual owner-bearing source/link records. Child tables lacking a tenant column must be checked through their parent; an orphan cannot supply a trustworthy tenant. Record audit-chain state and relevant existing evidence without treating a hash as proof of origin or a backup.
5. **Integrity and collisions:** report integrity and foreign-key check results, missing parents, incomplete revision chains, malformed or cross-tenant records, duplicate identifiers and keys under the actual schema, and all candidate-to-destination key collisions, including IDs repeated across owners. In the checked-in schema the portfolio key is `(canonical_owner_id, portfolio_id)`: the same ID under different owners can be two distinct portfolios, but an existing D02 destination with that ID is a re-key collision, including a tombstone. It is not permission to merge or choose a winner. Check restore receipt keys and owner-dependent fingerprints as well as parent/child keys.
6. **Stop and preserve:** before a proposed historical data batch, stop the whole batch for any unexplained candidate or foreign/cross-tenant inconsistency, collision, orphan, ambiguous ownership, incomplete dependency, conflicting provenance or affected restore receipt. The package’s technical recommendation stops on **any receipt** in scope, not merely one already shown non-invertible: only a later explicit disposition, evidenced safe retry semantics and a separately authorized feasible plan could resolve that stop; no such plan is established. A candidate with NULL lineage, as flagged in the D16 review, remains a stop until explicitly dispositioned from evidence; an intentionally nullable non-lineage provenance field is not automatically corrupt and remains unknown. Never fabricate a value to make a check pass. Record counts/digests/NULLs and unresolved cases in a proportionate local manifest; no external manifest service or invented signature is required.

This is an evidence specification, not permission or a command to inspect the database. The normal [IPC startup](../../src/ipc/entrypoint.ts) calls `openDatabase` and `ensureMigrations`; [the migration runner](../../src/persistence/migration_ledger.ts) can create a ledger and apply pending migrations. It is therefore not a read-only inventory path. A later inventory must use a genuinely read-only, consistent method with no startup migration, and establish its own no-mutation evidence; no verifier or such method was run here.

**4. Evidence available and missing.** Migrations [001](../../src/persistence/migrations/001_initial_schema.ts), [003](../../src/persistence/migrations/003_local_principal_bindings.ts), [004](../../src/persistence/migrations/004_portfolio_contribution_provenance.ts), [005](../../src/persistence/migrations/005_holding_security_identity.ts) and [006](../../src/persistence/migrations/006_portfolio_restore.ts) identify possible tables and constraints; the [ledger source](../../src/persistence/migration_ledger.ts) defines expected checksum behavior. No count, successful check or absence of rows is inferred.

**5. Dependencies and consequences.** D15 supplies the inclusion need and candidate proof; D14 preserves NULL/unknown evidence; D17 is conditional on a later re-key decision; D18/D19 cover migration and recovery if any change is separately authorized. The package requests a batch-wide stop and separately authorized read-only inventory; the [operating model](../IPD-OPERATING-MODEL.md#operating-context-and-authority) remains owner-directed for routine work, but it does not resolve D22’s specific D02 restriction. **6. Recommended disposition and exact proposed wording (not approved):**

Stop the whole proposed data batch on an unexplained or ambiguous candidate, collision, orphan, foreign/cross-tenant inconsistency, NULL lineage requiring disposition, incomplete history or any affected restore receipt; a receipt remains a stop until safe retry semantics are evidenced and separately dispositioned. Never guess, merge, overwrite, choose a winner or partially move. The present review makes no claim that any such row or conflict exists and authorizes no inventory or migration.

**7. Operator evidence and separate authority.** **Operator evidence: required** for an actual historical migration decision or completeness claim; the database and all results remain unknown now. **Separate decision: required** for D16’s formal disposition and the exact scope/authority of any future read-only D02 inventory; any subsequent restricted migration needs separate applicable authority after D22 is reconciled. **3. Minimum sufficient requirement.**

- **Preflight and preservation:** Before a future destructive, migration, replacement or otherwise difficult-to-reverse data action, require a verified, consistent recoverable backup or equivalent preservation copy and a usable restore path under the [operating model](../IPD-OPERATING-MODEL.md#proportionate-safeguards). - **Scope and atomic unit:** Prefer a new ordered, checksummed schema-only migration if one is actually needed and later approved; never edit migrations 001–006, and do not use a schema migration to seed/provision owner, relationship, provider, source or portfolio rows. Place any required parent unique keys before their composite FKs, enforce D03’s matching tenant references and restrictive deletes, and verify foreign keys on the relevant connection. A specifically approved compatible table rebuild may copy existing values without changing their domain meaning; it is not permission to seed, re-key or backfill. This unit does not include ledger setup executed before its transaction. Check the complete planned ledger/order before applying the first pending migration. If multiple migrations may be applied, define safe checkpoints/recovery for previously committed units or establish a separately tested atomic grouping; do not assume the current runner groups them. On a failed or indeterminate COMMIT/ROLLBACK, stop, preserve evidence and reconcile actual state under applicable authority; do not claim zero effect or blindly retry. Do not manually edit or rewind ledger entries to manufacture success.
- **Validation and recovery:** Under later applicable authority, validate the chosen SQLite features on disposable fresh/current-schema fixtures, including only relevant composite FKs/parent keys, `RESTRICT`, constraints, indexes, transaction/failure and rollback behavior; preserve S1/Direct P1 and recorded NULLs. Verify relevant schema, ledger, FK/integrity, counts/digests and durable readback/reopen after any permitted change; stop on mismatch. Apply D19’s backup/restore and post-commit limits. These are future checks, not tests performed here.

**4. The runner checks known entries and then checks order/checksums while iterating; a later already-recorded migration following a missing earlier one can be detected only after the earlier pending unit has committed. ## D19 — Rollback and recovery limits

**1. Exact source wording and references.** [Decision package D19](IPD-D02-PROGRAM-AUTHORITY-DECISION-PACKAGE-2026-11-16.md#d19--re-key-atomicity-rollback-and-recovery-cutoff); [applicability review D19](IPD-D02-LOCAL-SINGLE-USER-SCOPE-APPLICABILITY-REVIEW-2026-11-15.md#d19--essential-proportionate-rollback-and-recovery); [D14 reconciliation](IPD-D02-D11-D14-LOCAL-SINGLE-USER-RECONCILIATION-2026-11-15.md#d14--historical-sourceprovenance-with-null-or-no-link); [local tombstone restore contract](../LOCAL-PERSONAL-USE-PERSISTENCE.md#authenticated-tombstone-restoration).

> ### D19 — Re-key atomicity, rollback and recovery cutoff
>
> - **What / why:** Decide when a failed or post-commit migration can be reversed, and what happens after new D02 writes make reversal unsafe.
> - **Alternatives and consequences:** (a) one transaction, pre-commit rollback on any mismatch, reverse only before new/conflicting D02 writes; otherwise restore verified backup/reconcile under separate authority; (b) partial compensation; (c) unconditional inverse update. (b)/(c) can overwrite new writes or create split ownership.
> - **Technical recommendation — NOT APPROVED:** Atomic `BEGIN IMMEDIATE`, pre/post manifests, FK/count/digest checks and all-or-nothing rollback; post-commit reverse only if manifest proves no new D02 writes or conflict. Otherwise no partial reversal: use verified backup/recovery procedure and audit reconciliation under a separate approval. Never delete audit history or rewind migration ledger.
> - **Risk / S1 effect / approval:** Recovery may require downtime and backup reconciliation; a post-write rollback may be impossible without a recovery plan. No operation authorized. **Level C procedure approval; actual recovery/migration is Level E.**

**2. Applicability classification.** **ESSENTIAL**. Recovery protects a single local operator as well as a larger deployment. The detailed inverse-re-key cutoff is only relevant if a re-key is later selected; general data preservation, rollback awareness and a usable recovery path are still necessary independently of re-key. This report does not establish any working live recovery path.

**3. Minimum sufficient requirement.**

- **Before and during a change:** Preserve a verified consistent recoverable copy and demonstrate a usable restore path before destructive or difficult-to-reverse work. A confirmed rollback of the one open, uncommitted transaction can undo that unit; it cannot undo an earlier committed migration or operation. If COMMIT/ROLLBACK is uncertain, stop and preserve evidence; do not claim no effect or retry blindly. [D09’s proposed transaction contract](IPD-D02-D07-D10-LOCAL-SINGLE-USER-RECONCILIATION-2026-11-15.md#d09--transaction-consistency-and-concurrency) remains pending and must not be confused with proof of this migration runner’s behavior.
- **Conditional data batch, not current scope:** If a historical re-key is later selected, require a complete, evidenced preflight and pre/post count/key/digest/FK manifest for the approved candidate set, plus one feasible all-or-nothing data transaction on one connection. The package proposes `BEGIN IMMEDIATE`; its locking, FK and rollback feasibility would need later validation. Stop rather than split the batch or use an unapproved workaround if atomic movement cannot be demonstrated. The existing per-migration schema runner does not establish that data-batch guarantee.
- **Recorded history is not a full undo:** The checked-in [store](../../src/persistence/portfolio_store.ts) retains prior revision/holding snapshots, but its committed non-no-op RESET/REPLACE path deletes the then-current contribution set and creates a new one. The [broker importer](../../src/persistence/brokerImportPersistence.ts) separately refuses REPLACE for an existing portfolio. Earlier file-level metadata may no longer be in that set, and preserved revision digests or holdings do not prove it can be reconstructed; preserve existing W-10 semantics and treat missing evidence as unknown. The current model does not establish a general arbitrary-revision undo or complete file-to-holding recovery.
- **Tombstone restore is narrower than database recovery:** The documented v1 `portfolio.restore` accepts only `DEFAULT_PORTFOLIO`, an expected revision and an idempotency key under the authenticated owner/tenant. A new restore requires an eligible tombstone and `expectedRevision` matching the last-active pointer rather than the DELETE revision; it copies the last active holdings snapshot, appends a RESTORE revision/receipt/audit event and retains rows still present. An exact same-owner/tenant, key and revision retry returns the original receipt even after later lifecycle changes, without another revision or audit event; a changed request under the same key is rejected. The operation does not restore other portfolio IDs, reconstruct contribution rows already removed by reset/replacement, recover a lost/corrupt database, or prove a full rollback. Existing receipt fingerprints include owner/tenant, so any future owner-key movement must address retry behavior rather than silently changing keys.
- **After commit:** SQLite transaction rollback cannot undo a committed unit. If re-key is ever separately selected, assess a reverse mapping only with evidence proving no subsequent D02 writes, no destination conflict and no other affected changes that would make reversal unsafe, plus an invertible feasible plan and applicable authority; otherwise do not partially reverse. Preserve the current database/evidence and later writes before contemplating an older backup, and resolve how those later changes can be retained or reconciled. A backup contains only what it captured; restoring an older copy can discard newer portfolio, audit or ledger changes. If complete recovery cannot be established, stop and report the gap rather than claim success.
- **Audit/ledger and older backups:** The local [audit hash chain](../../src/persistence/audit.ts) is neither an independent backup nor proof against complete rewrite or truncation. Never manually delete or rewrite audit history or migration-ledger rows to simulate rollback. A whole-database restore from an older snapshot would also restore that snapshot’s older schema, ledger and audit state. How to reconcile that consequence with the package’s prohibition on rewinding the ledger/deleting audit history, later writes and application migration behavior requires an explicit, evidence-backed recovery decision; this report does not declare such a restore safe or permissible. Preserve the later state and evidence independently before any authorized recovery consideration.

**4. Evidence available and missing.** Migration [006](../../src/persistence/migrations/006_portfolio_restore.ts), the [store](../../src/persistence/portfolio_store.ts) and [local contract](../LOCAL-PERSONAL-USE-PERSISTENCE.md#authenticated-tombstone-restoration) describe the narrow restore, receipt and contribution-set behaviors; the [runner](../../src/persistence/migration_ledger.ts) describes per-migration commits. No live data, historical contribution set, backup, subsequent-write record, restore exercise, audit-chain result, actual ledger or SQLite failure behavior was inspected or tested. No historical metadata availability or successful four-portfolio recovery can be inferred.

**5. Dependencies and consequences.** D14 requires unknown provenance to stay unknown; D15/D16 remain unresolved; D17 re-key is deferred; D18 distinguishes migration units and checks. D03 identity/tenant references, S1/Direct P1 and the existing audit/ledger must be preserved. D22’s specific provisioning/migration restriction and applicable recovery scope remain independent. An inability to prove backup completeness, later-write reconciliation or ledger/audit treatment blocks a safe recovery claim and may block a proposed change; no enterprise recovery service or multi-actor ceremony is implied.

**6. Recommended disposition and exact proposed wording (not approved):**

> **D19 — AMEND (proposed):** Retain proportionate verified backup, preservation and usable recovery safeguards for future authorized data/schema work. Treat confirmed transaction rollback as undo of only its uncommitted unit; treat indeterminate outcomes and all post-commit changes as requiring evidence-based recovery, not automatic retry or assumed reversal. Preserve prior revisions and recorded provenance while recognizing that a committed non-no-op RESET/REPLACE clears the current contribution set and starts a new set, and that `portfolio.restore` is limited to `DEFAULT_PORTFOLIO` (a new restore requires an eligible tombstone), not lost prior contribution records or whole-database recovery. Permit consideration of reversal only with evidence proving no new D02 writes, no destination conflict and no other affected changes that would make reversal unsafe, plus an invertible feasible plan and applicable authority; otherwise preserve current evidence and decide a tested recovery/reconciliation plan before acting. Do not manually erase audit or ledger history; explicitly resolve how any older whole-database backup, later writes and the package’s audit/ledger preservation rule interact. No recovery or migration is authorized here.

Operator evidence and separate authority.** **Operator evidence: required** for an actual recovery or difficult-to-reverse change: a consistent verified copy and usable restore path, actual schema/ledger and owner/tenant state, affected revision/provenance/receipt evidence, and whether later writes or conflicts exist. **Separate decision: required** for formal D19 disposition and any restricted recovery/migration scope under applicable authority; the older-snapshot/audit/ledger interaction remains **UNRESOLVED — EVIDENCE REQUIRED**.

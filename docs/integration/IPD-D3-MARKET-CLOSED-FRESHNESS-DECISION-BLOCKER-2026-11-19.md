Weekends and exchange holidays do not consume the allowance unless an official exceptional live equity session occurred. This is not a proposal to pause Dhan quote age overnight.

This revision supersedes the earlier memo's combined intraday blockers and W-22 phase-test checklist. Company name or ticker alone is insufficient. Dual listings need two explicit mappings; count the calendar of the exchange that supplied the selected price. Never use a union/intersection of exchange calendars or switch venue silently. ## 4. Deterministic five-trading-day method and examples

**Decision proposal for owner endorsement, not implemented:** given qualified observation date D, exchange E, evaluation cutoff T and pinned evidence versions, let C(E,T) be the set of verified completed live equity trading dates by T. Then `age = count(d in C(E,T) where D < d)`. Exclude D; count distinct dates once. The established threshold is five. Proposed boundary expression is `age <= 5` within allowance and `age > 5` beyond allowance; record explicit endorsement of that inclusive comparison, without changing the threshold. Missing price, unresolved mapping, future/non-trading observation date, incomplete calendar coverage or uncertain completion yields UNKNOWN with reason, not a guessed count or freshness approval.

Use a daily evaluation snapshot with an explicit latest-completed-trading-date watermark, qualified independently of whether the selected stock has a new row. An in-progress date does not count yet; once it is verified completed it counts even if the latest stock price is still Friday's. No intraday polling or auction-window monitoring is required. If today's completion cannot be established, report an as-of prior-day result explicitly or UNKNOWN for the requested current cutoff; never silently declare today's session closed or extend the allowance because a report is late. The owner must approve that daily snapshot/completion-evidence convention.

The following are deterministic **conditional evidence fixtures**, not invented dated calendars: F denotes a verified Friday observation date, S1…S6 the explicitly verified subsequent completed trading dates in the pinned exchange calendar. They assert no actual holiday dates or unreviewed weekday trading status. All checks assume no newer valid stock close is available; otherwise select that newer observation first. Real dated anchors below are separately sourced.

| Case | Qualified calendar and evaluation condition | Age / result under proposal |
|---|---|---|
| Friday price checked Saturday | Calendar explicitly confirms no completed live equity date after F through Saturday cutoff. | 0; weekend consumes nothing. |
| Friday price checked Sunday | Calendar explicitly confirms Saturday and Sunday closed, no exceptional live date. | 0; not two calendar days. |
| Friday price checked Monday | Weekend confirmed closed; Monday verified open but not yet completed. After Monday is verified completed it is S1. | 0 before completion; 1 after completion if Friday is still latest. If Monday is a holiday, 0. |
| Price followed by exchange holiday | Holiday H is explicitly closed on E, not merely a clearing holiday; no other later completed date. | 0 through H; next verified completed live date S1 gives 1. |
| Price followed by exceptional weekend trading | Saturday closed; Sunday officially confirmed completed live equity date S1. | 0 on Saturday; 1 after Sunday completes, even though it is a weekend/holiday-labelled date. If a valid Sunday close is obtained, its own age is 0. |
| Five completed trading sessions after price | Exactly S1, S2, S3, S4, S5 after D are verified completed, irrespective of elapsed weekends/holidays. | 5; at threshold, within allowance under proposed inclusive boundary. S6 gives 6, beyond allowance. |
| Missing or uncertain coverage | Any date in the required interval has unresolved live/closed/completed status, or mapping/observation date is uncertain. | UNKNOWN with reason and versions; no weekday fallback, no seven-calendar-day substitute. |

# IPD D-3 — market-closed freshness decision: evidence and blocker

- **Evidence cut-off:** 2026-11-19
- **Disposition:** **BLOCKED — no market-closed freshness rule is accepted.**
- **Scope:** IPD D-3 evidence/decision only. This is not W-22 implementation or approval. IID L-033 is separate and is not used as authority or a dependency here.

## 1. Decision boundary

D-3 is meant to decide how Dhan market-data freshness behaves while the relevant cash-equity market is closed, including the exchange/session model, holiday source, and last-close ageing rule. The reviewed implementation and primary exchange sources describe important parts of that problem, but they do **not** support choosing a closed-period rule.

No D-3-specific authorized decision-maker or signed policy record was found in the reviewed IPD material. IPD owns the existing freshness implementation; that alone does not identify who may set its product/data policy. W-22 is downstream and cannot supply or silently decide this policy. Do not infer authority from IID L-033 or P01's unrelated methodology-authority rule.

Until a decision is signed, the existing elapsed-time semantics remain unchanged. This evidence record does not authorize a pause, reset, grace period, threshold change, calendar-based exception, or production behavior change.

## 2. Verified implementation facts

- `evaluateFreshness` is elapsed-time based and has no exchange-session/calendar input. `D01_QUOTES` uses a 900-second threshold; `D02_OHLCV` uses 86,400 seconds. At or below the threshold is `GOOD`; above it through twice the threshold is `STALE`; older is `UNAVAILABLE`/suppressed. Invalid time evidence is unavailable, and future timestamps clamp to age zero.
- Dhan cash-equity segment types are `NSE_EQ` and `BSE_EQ`. The reviewed Dhan quote/status scope is an exact company + segment + ISIN-series identity. The reviewed instrument mapping has no verified effective-dated CAS-eligibility field.
- CAS eligibility is security/date dependent and can depend on derivatives availability across exchanges, with exceptions. It cannot safely be inferred from `NSE_EQ` versus `BSE_EQ` or from one universal cash close.
- Dhan's provider `asOf` semantics have not been qualified here as a snapshot time, last-trade time, final close time, or auction-price dissemination time. `receivedAt` is a distinct ingest time and must not be substituted for it.
- Dhan's composed D02 freshness status is presently `NOT_AVAILABLE` / `UNKNOWN`; it does not infer freshness from a historical candle. P01 D10 is a specification for effective-dated venue/session metadata, not an implemented calendar.

Primary implementation references: `src/quality/freshness_evaluator.ts`; `src/providers/dhan/dhan_freshness_status.ts`; `src/providers/dhan/dhan_operational_controls.ts`; `src/providers/dhan/dhan_normalizer.ts`; `src/providers/dhan/dhan_market_data_service.ts`; `src/providers/dhan/dhan_dto.ts`; `src/providers/dhan/dhan_instrument_map.ts`; `docs/IPD-P5-FRESHNESS-HTTP.md`.

### Additional BSE revised rules affecting eligibility and the close

The full official BSE Notice 20261117-35 (two fetch chunks) states—not the unverified FAQ—that CAS eligibility follows derivative availability on any exchange and continues from the first through the last derivatives-trading date across exchanges, subject to stated exclusions. Its exceptions include securities not available for trading (including CDSL and BSE unless interoperable-segment trading is invoked), the ex-date of a scheme of arrangement when SPOS does not determine a price, and a market-wide index circuit-breaker halt for the rest of the day. The CAS cash reference is VWAP from 15:15–15:30; if no trade in that period, use the day's LTP; if no trade that day, the prior close; for corporate action, the adjusted close or base price. The band is ±3%. Equilibrium price uses maximum executable volume, then minimum absolute imbalance, then closeness to the reference, with the reference price as the midpoint tie-break. If no equilibrium is found, the reference price is the close; non-CAS close remains last-30-minute CTS VWAP. The notice lists the reference price, indicative equilibrium/matchable quantity, cumulative buy/sell, imbalance/side and indicative index as CAS-period dissemination, and says CAS and non-CAS closing prices are disseminated together. It does not give a fixed final-price timestamp or the FAQ's full trade-information boundary; the named `FAQs_Annexure` remains unverified. If an index circuit breaker halts the market for the remainder of the day, CAS is not held and the existing close method (last-30-minute VWAP or LTP, as applicable) applies. These are main-notice findings, not FAQ verification or a complete cross-venue early/aborted-session procedure.

## 4. Precise blocker

D-3 cannot accept a decision until the following are resolved together:

1. **Authorized decision:** name the IPD policy owner/signatory and record the authority under which that person selects the market-closed rule. No D-3 sign-off record was identified.
2. **Remaining source completeness and per-security close boundary:** The official current CAS product page and accessible October CAS FAQ v3.0 support the 15:15–15:35 current CAS phases, non-F&O CTS through 15:30, and the 2026-08-03 / 2026-09-07 implementation dates. The official CAS page and live Muhurat notice display 15:50–16:00 post-close; NSE's separate general Market Timings page still prints 15:40–16:00. CMTR/19013 proves only that 15:40–16:00 took effect in 2011, not that it governs now. The product page's current circular series is CMTR/72394, 73362, 73845 and 74466; of these, 73362.pdf was read, while the original ZIPs for 72394, 73845 and 74466 returned HTTP 500. The future CMTR/77355 ZIP also returned HTTP 500; the official CAS 2.0 FAQ summarizes its Dec 7 framework but is not a substitute for that circular. Preserve the 15:40/15:50 discrepancy and do not infer the governing amendment from a page or FAQ alone. The BSE Notice 20261117-35 is accessible and supports its own future schedule, but the separate `FAQs_Annexure` could not be retrieved; no BSE FAQ claims are verified. Keep the BSE attachment gap explicit. Continue the bounded calendar audit for later live amendments, special/short sessions, and early/aborted-session procedures across both venues. Then specify how an approved rule identifies each security's relevant event: CTS end, actual CAS order-entry closure, matching completion, or final-price dissemination. SEBI says final close/quantity dissemination follows matching “as soon as practicable,” not at a guaranteed fixed minute; the reviewed exchange sources do not establish a universal per-security dissemination timestamp.
3. **Dhan scope and timestamp contract:** qualify which Dhan cash-equity instruments are covered, how CAS eligibility is obtained and effective-dated across exchanges, and exactly what vendor `asOf` denotes. Segment labels alone do not establish the derivative-eligibility set or a final-close observation. Keep `asOf`, `receivedAt`, and evaluation time distinct.
4. **Ageing rule:** explicitly choose whether age is wall-clock, trading-session time, paused only after a verified final close, or another rule; specify how age resumes at pre-open/open and how an observation already stale before the close is treated. Keep D01/D02 thresholds unchanged unless separately authorized.
5. **Governed calendar and rights:** identify the authoritative calendar/session source, owner and update/review process, historical/effective-date coverage, exceptional-session handling, and permission to retain/use source material or a derived calendar in a versioned local artifact. No authorized, rights-reviewed calendar source was established. D114's static holiday baseline is incomplete and is not a Dhan calendar.
6. **D02 boundary:** state whether and how the closed-session rule applies to daily OHLCV. Until actual D02 observation/cache semantics exist, preserve `NOT_AVAILABLE` / `UNKNOWN`; do not infer freshness from a candle or calendar date.

Relevant contract evidence: `docs/p01/P01_TIMESTAMP_CURRENCY_UNIT_RULES.md` §§1–3 and 8 require explicit times, effective-dated D10 sessions, historical calendar accuracy, and P07 ownership of freshness. `docs/p01/P01_SCHEMA_CATALOG.md` §D10 is specification-only. `docs/p01/P01_DEPENDENCY_REGISTER.md` records freshness thresholds as a downstream P07 concern. These requirements do not designate a calendar source or D-3 signatory.

### Illustrative discriminator — not a decision

For an illustrative Friday `asOf` of **2026-11-13 15:30 IST** evaluated at **2026-11-16 09:00 IST**, wall-clock age is **65 h 30 min = 235,800 s**. Under today's elapsed-time evaluator, this is `UNAVAILABLE` for D01 (greater than 1,800 s) and `UNAVAILABLE` for D02 (greater than 172,800 s). This only reports the current formula; it does **not** decide whether the weekend should count, whether the Friday observation was a final close, or what result a session-aware rule should produce.

A second discriminator is the close boundary: under the current NSE schedule, CAS-eligible CTS ends at 15:15 and the V3.0 FAQ says the cash reference price is disseminated during the 15:15–15:20 transition; this is not the final CAS close. Non-F&O CTS continues through 15:30. Under the December 7 future framework, CAS starts at 15:30; matching starts after a random entry close between 15:38 and 15:40 and may continue through the scheduled 15:45 matching window. The future FAQ's 15:30–15:31 reference-price broadcast is likewise not the final close. SEBI says final-close/quantity dissemination follows matching as soon as practicable, and neither NSE FAQ supplies a universal per-security final-price timestamp. BSE's separate FAQ remains unobtained, so no FAQ-based BSE dissemination boundary is asserted. Freezing quote age at one segment-wide CTS close would therefore be unsupported.

## 5. W-22 acceptance criteria (requirements only; do not implement here)

W-22 must not start until D-3 has an authorized, explicit decision and the blockers above have a source-backed disposition. Its acceptance evidence must include:

1. **Governed local calendar:** a versioned, effective-dated local schedule with exchange/session/security scope, provenance, source revision or immutable reference, update owner, review procedure, and rights disposition. Runtime evaluation must have **no network dependency**. Missing, conflicting, out-of-horizon, or unqualified calendar/eligibility evidence must fail closed to the approved unknown/unavailable behavior; it must not fall back to a weekday heuristic or live fetch.
2. **Correct scope and history:** distinguish `NSE_EQ` and `BSE_EQ`, per-security CAS eligibility, cross-exchange derivative availability, exceptions, normal/holiday/special/short sessions, and relevant session phases. Preserve historical effective dates; never apply today's calendar retroactively to PIT evaluation. Include both the pre-2026-12-07 and post-2026-12-07 rule sets only when supported by official, reconciled exchange instructions.
3. **Qualified timestamp semantics:** prove what Dhan quote `asOf` represents and keep `asOf`, `receivedAt`, and evaluation time separate. Do not use receipt time or a guessed daily close to manufacture an exchange observation.
4. **One freshness authority:** `evaluateFreshness` remains the sole owner of the approved time/session-age calculation. Dhan cache classification and every existing freshness consumer must use that result; no duplicate, divergent session logic. Preserve existing suppression, invalid/future timestamp behavior, and D01/D02 thresholds unless a separately authorized change says otherwise.
5. **Deterministic boundary tests:** test pre-open, in-session, CTS-to-CAS, each CAS phase, final-price availability, post-close, weekend, ordinary holiday, special Sunday/Muhurat or other exceptional session, early/aborted session where applicable, and the first session after a holiday. Test exact phase/threshold boundaries, the 2026-12-07 effective-date cutover, and distinct NSE/BSE outcomes. Every expected verdict must be tied to the signed D-3 rule and a versioned calendar input.
6. **No surprise transitions:** prove there are no verdict flips caused solely by crossing calendar/session phases except the explicitly approved transitions; in-session age progression remains deterministic. Include stale-before-close and missing/ambiguous eligibility cases.
7. **D02 preservation:** prove a missing D02 observation remains `NOT_AVAILABLE` / `UNKNOWN` and no candle, weekend, or holiday is treated as an inferred freshness observation. If D02 semantics are extended, test them against its explicitly approved daily rule without silently changing the existing status contract.
8. **Regression and evidence:** show quote cache/status, `/v1/freshness`, suppression/eligibility, and downstream consumers preserve existing contracts; include source/version/hash and test output. Do not add a scheduler, refresh trigger, provider call, or runtime network dependency as a side effect of this work.

These are acceptance requirements, not permission to implement W-22 or to select the missing policy by implication.

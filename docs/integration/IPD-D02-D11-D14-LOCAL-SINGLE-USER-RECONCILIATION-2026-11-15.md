## D11 — Broker-account identity and normalization

**1. Exact existing register wording and sources.** [Decision package D11](IPD-D02-PROGRAM-AUTHORITY-DECISION-PACKAGE-2026-11-16.md#d11--broker-account-identity-and-normalization); [applicability review D11](IPD-D02-LOCAL-SINGLE-USER-SCOPE-APPLICABILITY-REVIEW-2026-11-15.md#d11--defer-to-future-scope-account-identity-tokens); [technical proposal §13.3](IPD-D02-OWNERSHIP-RELATIONSHIP-TECHNICAL-DESIGN-2026-11-15.md#133-provider-and-account-reference-normalization).

> ### D11 — Broker-account identity and normalization
>
> - **What / why:** Decide what stable key distinguishes a broker source stream without storing account numbers, usernames, credentials or tokens. The current authority does not specify provider account-ID formats or same-account cross-import deduplication.
> - **Alternatives and consequences:** (a) server-issued opaque canonical UUIDv4 stable for one provider/account stream; (b) store normalized provider account identifiers, adding sensitive-data and provider-specific normalization risk; (c) random token per import, which cannot recognize a continuing source stream; (d) provenance digest alone, which identifies an artifact, not necessarily an account.
> - **Technical recommendation — NOT APPROVED:** Use a server-owned opaque UUID token stable across imports for the same source stream; reject raw account/credential strings. Ask PA to confirm whether that provenance-level token is sufficient or provider-specific evidence/deduplication is required before asserting two streams are the same account.
> - **Risk / S1 effect / approval:** Token issuance/stability and cross-file deduplication remain unspecified. No S1 effect. **Level C and provider-identity policy approval requested; no real token/account lookup is authorized.**

**2. Current single-user applicability.** The four illustrative portfolios identify one Dhan origin, one Zerodha origin and two distinct manual portfolios. Neither the approved product direction nor the reviewed local scope establishes multiple accounts at one broker, a need to prove that two exports belong to the same account, or account-level deduplication. A provider value identifies a provider, not an account. A filename, symbol, holding match, timestamp, content hash or lineage digest does not by itself prove two artifacts came from the same—or different—account.

This does **not** remove existing file/content idempotency. The [local persistence contract §3](../LOCAL-PERSONAL-USE-PERSISTENCE.md#3-deliberate-projection-scope-contributions-vs-importevidence) describes the broker file content digest and recorded provenance. In the [store’s v1 write path](../../src/persistence/portfolio_store.ts), the MERGE duplicate check consults contribution IDs within the same owner and portfolio, with a separate latest-revision digest check and existing revision checks. It is not a cross-portfolio or account-matching service; a non-no-op RESET/REPLACE starts a new contribution set, so the ledger alone is not proof of history-wide deduplication.

**3. Minimum sufficient requirement.** Preserve the recorded provider, file name, content and lineage digests, import/contribution times, semantics, and per-holding/manual evidence when present; preserve NULL where a fact was not recorded. Retain the existing content-based duplicate/no-op, revision, authorization and qualification rules within their current scope. Do not introduce broker account IDs, source-stream tokens or account-level matching, and do not add credentials, API tokens, usernames or raw account identifiers to provenance or audit metadata. Do not merge portfolios or claim two imports represent one account merely because their provider or evidence resembles each other.

**4. Complexity to simplify or defer.** Defer UUID issuance/stability and lookup, provider-specific account normalization, account-key administration and account-level deduplication. Concrete reasons to revisit would be a real need to distinguish two accounts at the same broker, match exports across dates or portfolios to one account, or combine separately identified streams in one portfolio. A documented reliable provider account-identity contract could supply evidence for such a decision, but evidence alone neither establishes a current requirement nor authorizes storage, lookup or a particular design. No future account requirement is added now.

**5. Dependencies.** D01–D03 keep identity/tenant and ownership separate from broker source. D04–D06’s relationship safeguards and D07–D09’s access/transaction proposals remain pending. [D10’s proposed source mapping](IPD-D02-D07-D10-LOCAL-SINGLE-USER-RECONCILIATION-2026-11-15.md#d10--provider-representation-and-catalog), D12/D13’s portfolio-origin and provenance choices, and D14’s unknown-history treatment are not account-identity decisions. D22 does not authorize an account lookup or the restricted provisioning/migration operations.

**6. Exact recommended decision wording (proposed, not approved):**

> **D11 — DEFER TO FUTURE SCOPE (proposed):** Do not require an opaque broker-account/source-stream token, account identifier, cross-file account match or account-level deduplication for the current four-portfolio local scope. Preserve recorded provider, file, content/lineage and time evidence and the existing portfolio-scoped content-based no-op and revision rules; do not store credentials or raw account identifiers as provenance. Do not infer account sameness or difference from provider, filename, symbols or digests. Revisit account identity only if an actual multi-account or cross-file account-matching need and reliable evidence are established under applicable direction. This proposal does not alter existing supported import paths or authorize any account lookup.

**7. Outstanding evidence and consequences.** Provider account-identity formats, token issuance/stability, reliable cross-file matching, actual account multiplicity and live import history were not established. The checked-in import validator’s request/holding source comparison is not account attestation. Operator-supplied filenames have not been inspected for sensitive information; this report neither assumes they are secret-free nor authorizes rewriting them. File-level no-op evidence cannot answer account-level questions, especially across portfolios or after a contribution-set reset; do not assert same-account deduplication or silently consolidate on that basis. D11 remains **PENDING**.

## D12 — Portfolio source cardinality and active uniqueness

**1. Exact existing register wording and sources.** [Decision package D12](IPD-D02-PROGRAM-AUTHORITY-DECISION-PACKAGE-2026-11-16.md#d12--portfolio-source-cardinality-and-active-uniqueness); [applicability review D12](IPD-D02-LOCAL-SINGLE-USER-SCOPE-APPLICABILITY-REVIEW-2026-11-15.md#d12--simplify-source-cardinality-and-uniqueness); [technical proposal §13.4](IPD-D02-OWNERSHIP-RELATIONSHIP-TECHNICAL-DESIGN-2026-11-15.md#134-portfolio-source-cardinality-uniqueness-and-row-links).

> ### D12 — Portfolio source cardinality and active uniqueness
>
> - **What / why:** Decide how many source records a portfolio may have, including whether hybrid portfolios and multiple streams of one provider are allowed, and what active tuple is unique.
> - **Alternatives and consequences:** (a) zero-or-more over lifecycle, with one active source per `(tenant, owner, portfolio, provider, opaque account ref)`; manual uses `MANUAL_PERSONAL`/NULL and is unique per portfolio; (b) exactly one source per portfolio; (c) unrestricted duplicates. (b) rules out explicit hybrid/import provenance; (c) permits duplicate active origins.
> - **Technical recommendation — NOT APPROVED:** Use (a), with partial unique index; revoked source remains historical and cannot receive new links. Distinct portfolios remain distinct even when provider is the same.
> - **Risk / S1 effect / approval:** Hybrid workflows add validation complexity and may need provider-level duplicate-account rules. No S1 effect. **Level C approval requested, including manual/hybrid policy.**

**2. Current single-user applicability.** Dhan, Zerodha, Manual A and Manual B each have a distinct portfolio identity and one stated origin in the example; the two manual portfolios share a source class but must not share an ID or history. This does not establish a universal ban on hybrid portfolios, a one-portfolio-per-provider rule, or a real account stream. [Migration 001](../../src/persistence/migrations/001_initial_schema.ts) keys a portfolio by `(canonical_owner_id, portfolio_id)` and stores `tenant_id` separately; tenant matching and scoped access are still required. It does not contain a portfolio-origin column.

**3. Minimum sufficient requirement.** For these four designated portfolios, use at most one unambiguous established canonical origin per portfolio for source-specific writes, not one source record per import. Preserve uniqueness of each portfolio ID under its owner and validate the tenant and exact target in the authorized context. A small optional origin value on the parent, or an equivalently scoped server-owned mapping if needed, can have at most one authoritative value per portfolio without a provider/account tuple index; its representation is pending D13 and detailed-schema review. The same provider or manual kind may occur in different portfolios. A non-no-op committed import writes its evidence to the current contribution set; duplicate content follows the existing no-op rules rather than creating a second entry, and later RESET/REPLACE can replace the set. Neither creates duplicate portfolio identities or implies account equality.

Do not force an unknown origin to a fabricated value. As proposed in [D10](IPD-D02-D07-D10-LOCAL-SINGLE-USER-RECONCILIATION-2026-11-15.md#d10--provider-representation-and-catalog), a genuinely empty, unclassified portfolio with **no source-bearing history**, if available under an applicable path, may have its first qualified source bound atomically with that write. A currently empty snapshot or empty contribution set alone does not prove that earlier source-bearing history never existed. If the selected portfolio has historical source evidence but its origin is unknown or conflicting, retain the evidence and refuse a source-sensitive write until reliable origin evidence and applicable authority exist; authorized historical reads need not invent a classification. Retain other supported broker paths, including the recognized Groww adapter where applicable, without treating the example as a permanent two-broker limit.

**4. Complexity to simplify or defer.** Defer lifecycle source rows, partial uniqueness over `(provider, account token)`, per-stream revocation and general hybrid policy unless an actual supported workflow needs them. Do not enforce a global uniqueness constraint on source provider or content digest, collapse Manual A/B, or add a permanent hybrid prohibition. Existing mixed or uncertain historical evidence must not be erased to fit the example’s single-origin assumption.

**5. Dependencies.** Approved D01–D03 establish distinct S1/D02 identity, tenant consistency and relationship references, not source-record cardinality. D07–D09 propose the protected owner/portfolio boundary and transaction; D10 proposes the controlled provider mapping and trustworthy origin check. All remain pending. D11 defers account matching; D13 chooses any origin representation; D14–D16 address unknown history and inventory. All these pending decisions remain independent of D21/D22 authority.

**6. Exact recommended decision wording (proposed, not approved):**

> **D12 — AMEND (proposed):** For the four distinct local portfolio examples, retain owner-scoped unique portfolio IDs and at most one established, unambiguous canonical origin per portfolio; validate each source-specific write against it and refuse mismatches rather than silently changing the origin. Manual A and Manual B remain separate even though their source class is the same. Preserve tenant matching, other supported provider paths, recorded evidence of distinct committed imports and the existing portfolio-scoped content no-op. An unknown or conflicting historical origin is not a source assignment: preserve it and refuse source-sensitive mutation until trustworthy evidence exists. Where an otherwise permitted empty portfolio has no source-bearing history, its first qualified source-specific write may bind an origin atomically. Do not require account-stream tuple indexes or one source row per import, globally limit a provider to one portfolio, erase historical evidence, or treat this example as a general hybrid ban. The physical origin representation remains a pending D13/schema choice.

**7. Outstanding evidence and consequences.** A trustworthy target-origin fact for all existing portfolios, its representation, and end-to-end source validation are not established; no live portfolio/source history was inspected. The checked-in store contains `createEmptySnapshot` among v2 primitives described as synthetic and disconnected from product dispatch, while the documented v1 personal `portfolio.create` requires non-empty holdings ([store](../../src/persistence/portfolio_store.ts); [local persistence §1](../LOCAL-PERSONAL-USE-PERSISTENCE.md#1-what-it-enables)). Thus the empty-unclassified case is a design possibility where a permitted path exists, not a claim that the personal UI currently offers it or authority to add one. If reliable target-origin evidence is unavailable, do not enable a source-specific write by guessing. D12 remains **PENDING**.

## D13 — Root source registry versus derived evidence; row-level links

**1. Exact existing register wording and sources.** [Decision package D13](IPD-D02-PROGRAM-AUTHORITY-DECISION-PACKAGE-2026-11-16.md#d13--root-source-registry-versus-derived-evidence-row-level-links); [applicability review D13](IPD-D02-LOCAL-SINGLE-USER-SCOPE-APPLICABILITY-REVIEW-2026-11-15.md#d13--simplify-root-source-registry-and-link-tables); [design acceptance findings §§4–5](IPD-D02-OWNERSHIP-RELATIONSHIP-DESIGN-ACCEPTANCE-REVIEW-2026-11-15.md#5-specific-schema-and-implementation-risks-found).

> ### D13 — Root source registry versus derived evidence; row-level links
>
> - **What / why:** Decide whether a portfolio-level source registry is required or whether source should be derived from existing holding/contribution evidence. If a registry is kept, decide how every new holding revision and import contribution binds to it and how mismatches are refused.
> - **Alternatives and consequences:** (a) proposed root `portfolio_source_records` plus restrictive per-holding-revision and per-contribution link tables; (b) derive source only from existing row evidence, avoiding a root table but making portfolio-level origins/cardinality less explicit; (c) root table without links, which permits a Dhan root to disagree with Zerodha/manual rows.
> - **Technical recommendation — NOT APPROVED:** Keep the root registry and both link tables; each new row has exactly one link, written in the same transaction; validate provider/source against `source_broker`/contribution evidence. Permit mixed-source portfolios only with distinct explicit records and links.
> - **Risk / S1 effect / approval:** Additional schema and historical link treatment are required. Existing provenance must not be overwritten. **Level C approval requested.**

**2. Current single-user applicability.** [Migration 001](../../src/persistence/migrations/001_initial_schema.ts) records each holding’s `source_broker` and `lineage_digest` under its owner/portfolio/revision key; [migration 004](../../src/persistence/migrations/004_portfolio_contribution_provenance.ts) adds nullable contribution file, broker, content/lineage digest, import-time and semantics fields. The [manual mapper](../../src/persistence/manualPersonalPortfolio.ts) records `manual-personal` and `MANUAL_PERSONAL_ENTRY`. The [local projection contract §3](../LOCAL-PERSONAL-USE-PERSISTENCE.md#3-deliberate-projection-scope-contributions-vs-importevidence) keeps missing values NULL rather than fabricating them. These fields are repository evidence of a useful starting point, not proof that every live record is complete or that every holding can be linked to a specific import file.

The [broker importer](../../src/persistence/brokerImportPersistence.ts) checks that each request holding’s source matches the request’s source. That agreement alone proves neither that the request is independently attested nor that the selected portfolio’s intended origin matches it. The existing contribution table references its portfolio, not a particular revision or holding; do not invent a file-to-holding join from matching labels or digests.

**3. Minimum sufficient requirement.**

- Retain each portfolio’s existing owner/tenant, distinct portfolio ID, revision and child keys, and all recorded holding, contribution and manual evidence. D03’s approved tenant-scoped **relationship** FKs and status checks remain necessary; a source representation does not replace ownership authorization.
- Use D10’s proposed small controlled source mapping, preserving existing historical labels including `manual-personal` without rewriting them. Compare qualified incoming evidence and relevant retained evidence against a trustworthy server-owned origin fact for the selected portfolio in the same authorized transaction. Request-to-row equality, provider alone, a filename hint, symbol or owner equality is not such proof.
- If existing evidence truly establishes the target origin, reuse it; if it cannot, a single durable portfolio-origin value or equally small server-owned mapping **may** be needed. Neither choice is approved here. Unknown/conflicting origin means preserve history and refuse source-sensitive writes, not guess; where permitted, an empty portfolio with no source-bearing history may bind its first qualified source atomically as described in D12. Authorized historical reads may expose recorded facts and unknowns without fabricating the missing fact.
- Preserve the recorded provenance at its actual granularity. The current schema does not establish an exact contribution-to-revision/holding association for every historical row; a parent-origin field would not supply that proof. If a demonstrated requirement needs stronger file-to-row lineage, evaluate the smallest evidence-backed extension under applicable authority instead of claiming the existing labels or a new origin value provide it.

**4. Complexity to simplify or defer.** Do not require `portfolio_source_records`, a provider catalog and both `portfolio_holding_source_links` and `portfolio_contribution_source_links` merely to express the four single-origin examples. Defer dynamic registration, source-revocation administration, graph identifiers and per-row link lifecycle unless real hybrid, multiple-stream or stronger lineage needs demonstrate their necessity. This is not a recommendation to delete existing provenance, deny other supported brokers, or silently treat a simple origin fact as equivalent to an auditable row-link graph.

**5. Dependencies.** D01–D03 and the approved architecture govern owner/tenant identity independently of source. D06’s existing-audit recommendation and D07–D09’s complete access boundary and same-transaction proposals are pending; D10 controls the proposed source mapping, D11 defers account identity, and D12 states the example’s cardinality. D14 governs missing historical evidence; D15–D19 and D22 constrain any future inventory, schema or data action.

**6. Exact recommended decision wording (proposed, not approved):**

> **D13 — AMEND (proposed):** Preserve portfolio owner/tenant/ID, revisions, holding-level source/lineage/manual facts and the contribution provenance actually recorded, including NULLs. Reuse that evidence and a small controlled source mapping; require a trustworthy server-owned origin fact for the selected portfolio and compare qualified source evidence in the authorized transaction. If existing evidence cannot establish the origin, a single portfolio-level value or equally small scoped mapping may be considered under the pending schema decision; until trustworthy origin exists, refuse source-sensitive mutation rather than infer it. Allow first binding only for an otherwise permitted empty portfolio with no source-bearing history. Do not require a root source registry, provider-admin lifecycle or two per-row link tables solely for the current four-portfolio examples. Preserve other supported broker paths and do not claim that a portfolio-origin fact proves individual holding-to-file lineage; reconsider minimal representation if a demonstrated provenance need exceeds the retained evidence.

**7. Outstanding evidence and consequences.** There is no demonstrated complete server-owned target-origin attestation or non-bypassable D02 source-check path; current request/holding agreement is insufficient. No live NULL, conflicting or mixed-source row inventory was available or inspected here, and the current contribution structure does not prove every historical file-to-holding association. The [store](../../src/persistence/portfolio_store.ts) replaces the current contribution set on a non-no-op RESET/REPLACE; preserved revision snapshots alone do not prove the removed file metadata can be reconstructed. The broker importer separately refuses REPLACE for an existing portfolio. Whether the smaller representation preserves all required historical provenance across those operations is unresolved; retain the existing active-set semantics and resolve any evidence-retention gap before claiming complete provenance. D13 remains **PENDING**.

## D14 — Historical source/provenance with NULL or no link

**1. Exact existing register wording and sources.** [Decision package D14](IPD-D02-PROGRAM-AUTHORITY-DECISION-PACKAGE-2026-11-16.md#d14--historical-sourceprovenance-with-null-or-no-link); [applicability review D14](IPD-D02-LOCAL-SINGLE-USER-SCOPE-APPLICABILITY-REVIEW-2026-11-15.md#d14--essential-historical-provenance-remains-unknown-when-absent); [historical treatment in the technical proposal](IPD-D02-OWNERSHIP-RELATIONSHIP-TECHNICAL-DESIGN-2026-11-15.md#9-historical-record-compatibility-migration-and-rollback-proposal).

> ### D14 — Historical source/provenance with NULL or no link
>
> - **What / why:** Decide how existing contribution/holding records that predate the source registry are displayed and whether any are backfilled. Migration 004 deliberately left some historical import provenance NULL; no operational rows have been inspected.
> - **Alternatives and consequences:** (a) leave NULL/unlinked as `UNKNOWN` until evidence-backed disposition; (b) backfill from reliable original import records; (c) infer from symbol, portfolio name or likely broker. (c) fabricates provenance; (b) requires evidence review and scope.
> - **Technical recommendation — NOT APPROVED:** Preserve current values and label unknown/unlinked; only evidence-backed, separately authorized corrections may create links. Never infer from symbol/name or normalize away existing NULLs.
> - **Risk / S1 effect / approval:** Historical provenance can remain incomplete in D02 listings. S1/Direct P1 is unaffected. **Level C approval for treatment; each future correction needs its own operational authorization.**

**2. Current single-user applicability.** Unrecorded provenance cannot safely be recovered from a portfolio name, symbol, expected broker, current owner or the fact there is only one local user. Migration 004 adds nullable fields without populating historical rows; that describes schema behavior **if** such rows existed, not evidence that any operational row is NULL. Migration 001 declares holding source/lineage non-NULL, but does not provide the proposed source-link tables. “Unlinked” is a treatment for records if such a representation is later adopted, not a claim that missing links or even those tables exist in the operational database. No operational rows were inspected.

**3. Minimum sufficient requirement.**

- **Unknown stays unknown:** retain existing NULLs, unlinked status if applicable, IDs, revisions, digests and recorded source/manual facts; present absent facts as unknown without writing a literal replacement or guessing from names, symbols, source similarity or current assignment. Do not infer portfolio ownership from broker/source. A later correction or link must be based on specific reliable original evidence, preserve the prior evidence and original unknown in the trace of the change, and occur only under applicable explicit authority; none is authorized here.
- **Owner and historical consistency:** preserve S1 and Direct P1, D02’s distinct owner and D03’s matching tenant/composite relationship references, existing portfolio/child keys, revision history, restore receipts and audit history. D07–D09’s proposed D02-specific protected, freshly resolved owner boundary and transaction/snapshot discipline are not yet implemented or approved. Do not silently re-key, merge, overwrite or expose S1-owner history as D02; D15/D16 remain unresolved. For any later contemplated movement, inventory the actual affected owner-key records, provenance and receipt/idempotency dependencies; stop on collision, orphan, cross-tenant inconsistency or ambiguous ownership rather than selecting a winner or partially moving history.
- **Recoverability:** before a destructive, migration, replacement or otherwise difficult-to-reverse data operation, use a verified recoverable backup or equivalent preservation copy, confirm its integrity and that a restore path is usable, as required by the [operating model’s proportionate safeguards](../IPD-OPERATING-MODEL.md#proportionate-safeguards). The copy must be a consistent representation of the actual SQLite state, including journal state as needed, and cover the applicable schema/migration ledger, S1/ownership data, portfolios, revisions, holdings, contributions, restore receipts, audit and any source evidence actually present. Exact backup mechanics and recovery evidence are not established here. If preservation, integrity or restoration cannot be established, stop rather than claim a safe change.
- **Atomicity and audit:** preserve existing revision, idempotency and fail-closed checks; for any later authorized change, use the applicable one-transaction rule, check owner/tenant and relevant keys, counts, digests, NULLs, foreign-key integrity and receipt dependencies, and roll back a confirmed pre-commit failure. Preserve the existing append-only hash chain and migration ledger; append appropriate evidence rather than rewriting prior audit events. The local hash chain is neither an independent backup nor proof against complete rewrite or truncation. An unknown commit/rollback outcome is not a confirmed no-effect result.

The existing [tombstone restore contract](../LOCAL-PERSONAL-USE-PERSISTENCE.md#authenticated-tombstone-restoration) is limited to `DEFAULT_PORTFOLIO`: it retains the rows present at restore time, copies the last active holdings snapshot and records a receipt/audit event. It is not a database-backup restore, does not prove recovery for the other example portfolio IDs, and does not recreate contribution rows already removed by an earlier reset/replacement.

**4. Complexity to simplify or defer.** D14 does not require a source registry, guessed backfill, external audit system, enterprise recovery process, multi-actor approval chain or immediate owner-key movement. Preserve the existing W-10 active contribution-set semantics rather than silently changing reset/replacement behavior. If required historical evidence would otherwise be lost, determine a proportionate compatible preservation/retention approach under applicable authority; this report neither selects a new mechanism nor claims the gap is already solved.

**5. Dependencies.** D01–D03 are approved; D04–D10’s relationship, access, transaction, audit and source recommendations remain pending. D11–D13 define deferred account identity, per-example cardinality and any source representation. D15/D16 remain **UNRESOLVED — EVIDENCE REQUIRED** for historical disposition and live inventory; D17’s re-key scope, D18’s migration mechanics and D19’s rollback/recovery proposals remain pending. D22’s specific operational restriction remains unresolved; the general owner-directed operating model does not override it.

**6. Exact recommended decision wording (proposed, not approved):**

> **D14 — APPROVE with clarification (proposed):** Preserve historical source/provenance values, IDs, revisions and ownership evidence; keep absent or unlinked facts unknown and never infer them from names, symbols, provider or a current portfolio assignment. Evidence-backed correction or linking, if ever needed, must preserve prior evidence, be traceable and have applicable explicit authorization. Keep S1/Direct P1 unchanged, retain the D03 tenant-scoped relationship requirements and validate owner/tenant/portfolio and child consistency; do not silently re-key, merge or overwrite historical portfolios. Preserve revision, idempotency, audit and recovery safeguards, including a verified consistent recoverable copy and usable restore path before destructive, migration, replacement or otherwise difficult-to-reverse data actions. Retain current active contribution-set semantics while resolving any demonstrated historical-retention gap; neither the audit chain nor default-portfolio tombstone restore substitutes for a verified database recovery path.

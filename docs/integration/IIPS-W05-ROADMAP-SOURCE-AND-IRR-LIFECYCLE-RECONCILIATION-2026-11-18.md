## 5. Points owed to IRR

Each is a difference from the roadmap text or a statement the roadmap needs and the handoff does not make. None can be settled from IPD, because the IRR code and records are not readable here.

**C1. The denominator is 64, not 65 (rows 9 and 13).**
The roadmap requires W05 to "reconcile to W04". The W04 census of record is `DHAN_PORTFOLIO` revision 1: **64 holding rows, all active, 1 with a stored security identity, 63 without** ([W04 record](IIPS-W04-EVIDENCE-RECONCILIATION-AND-ACCEPTANCE-ASSESSMENT-2026-11-16.md), host output on `main`) (R). The handoff gives "0/65 holdings" from an IRR record of 2026-11-12, which is four days older than that measurement (IR). In IPD records, 65 is the number of in-universe rows of a tracked 68-row file, and those records state that this file is not established as the input of revision 1: "The counts are close and they are not equal" ([assessment](IIPS-W04-C4-C5-IDENTIFIER-SUPPLY-SOURCE-AND-OWNER-ASSESSMENT-2026-11-14.md), section 4.3) (R). Whether the IRR 65 has that origin is not known here. **Owed:** IRR states where 65 comes from, and restates W05 coverage against the 64 rows of revision 1. The numerator 0 is not affected.

**C2. θF is a portfolio ratio, not a dataset qualification threshold (row 12).**
Section K defines it (line 334): the number of eligible holding rows for which identity passes and "qualified, rights-permitted issuer filings/dataset contain all mandatory validated financial inputs for the applicable analysis and period/basis", divided by the eligible count `|E|`, must be at least θF. It is one number for the whole portfolio, and it gates W20. The handoff describes a "qualification-threshold mechanism" inside "per-issuer dataset qualification" and calls its value θF. **Owed:** IRR confirms which quantity `W05-THF-PROPOSAL-1` is. If it is the portfolio ratio of section K, the name is right. If it is a pass mark for one issuer's dataset, it is a different quantity and should not carry the name θF. Whether one dataset qualifies is, in the roadmap, a yes or no on stated conditions, not a threshold.

**C3. θF is established from evidence, it cannot be established yet, and W05 does not wait for it (row 12).**
The roadmap's process (line 345): W03 to W06 supply the census and per-row evidence, W10 to W14 supply the mandatory inputs per method, the engine outcomes and "the consequences of omissions"; then explicit numeric thresholds are recorded with the exposure left uncovered, the materiality, and the reason each gap is tolerable. "The standing PA grant covers these in-scope decisions; no additional human approval cycle is introduced." Line 347: "no defensible numeric θI, θF, θA or θM can be asserted now." Three consequences (D):

- The roadmap's state for θF is "not yet established". It has no state for a value that is proposed and awaits approval. A numeric proposal made before the W10 to W14 evidence exists has no basis the roadmap accepts, whoever approves it.
- θF blocks **W20**. It is not an acceptance condition of W05. The handoff's sentence that W05 cannot be complete while "the θF decision" is outstanding is stricter than the roadmap. W05 waits for its own deliverable and acceptance evidence only: the dossier, the rights verification and the reconciliation to W04.
- When θF is established, it is recorded in section K of the roadmap, in IPD. That recording is an IPD-side action. The decision itself is the Principal's under the standing grant.

**Owed:** IRR aligns the wording of `threshold.ts` and of its records with this, or shows a governing source that says otherwise. No decision is requested from the Principal now.

**C4. Identity comes first, row by row (row 13).**
A row counts toward the financial ratio only if its identity passes. With one stored identity in revision 1, **the financial numerator for revision 1 is at most 1, whatever datasets are provisioned** (D, from line 334 and the W04 census). A dataset shared by several positions of one issuer supports each position "only if each position's own identity and applicable method are qualified" (line 329). The roadmap forbids identity by symbol alone (W04), and the security to issuer bridge is W08. IPD stores a company-level resolution (`RESOLVED`, `CANONICAL_P04`) on all 64 rows, which is not a security identity (R). **Owed:** IRR confirms how its lifecycle maps a holding to an issuer, that a holding without a stored security identity is reported as not covered for the ratio, and that no mapping rests on a symbol or a name. Provisioning can still be planned by issuer before the identities exist; it does not raise the ratio until they do.

**C5. Sector and method coverage, and the source of the required metrics (rows 7 and 14).**
The deliverable names three axes: issuer, sector and method. The handoff names issuer and portfolio reporting. "Method" is the analysis a dataset can feed, and its mandatory inputs are fixed by W10 (the per-engine matrix), which depends on W05 and W07 and is not established. **Owed:** IRR confirms that the report can state coverage by sector and by method, and that its "required-metric" set is marked provisional until W10 fixes it.

**C6. Retention (row 3).**
The roadmap asks to verify "entitlement and retention where applicable", and the ownership register asks for "permissible use/retention" (line 285). **Owed:** IRR confirms that the rights record holds a retention term per source and that an unknown retention term fails closed like an unknown entitlement.

**C7. Ownership (row 10).**
The handoff's section 3 is titled "What the IPD work owns next". By the roadmap, W05 is executed by "IRR financial data and host/source operator", with "IPD boundaries intact", and repository responsibilities are "IRR for UI, analysis and reports; IPD for portfolio, identity, provider and freshness" (line 282). Financial filings, their rights, their ingestion and any coverage report are IRR and operator work. Section 7 lists what IPD does own.
